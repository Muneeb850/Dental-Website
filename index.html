FAQ

Common questions.

Do I need a referral to book?

No. You can book directly — new patients are always welcome.

Will treatment be painful?

We aim for comfortable care. Local anaesthetic and gentle technique are used, and you can pause at any time.

How often should I have a check-up?

Most patients benefit from a check-up and hygiene visit every six months; we will tailor this to you.

Do you treat dental emergencies?

Yes. Call us and we will arrange a same-day assessment where possible.

Can I see costs before I commit?

Always. You receive a clear written plan and estimate before any treatment starts.
